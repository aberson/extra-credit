Math operations and activities

Let a parent ask Dry Math for carrying and borrowing or for multiplication and exact division by fact family, and add one new activity, Number Bonds, without raising any quantity ceiling. An independent arithmetic checker is in place before any content changes, and every later step has to satisfy it.

This plan starts from the main that plan 01 leaves behind: worksheet-first controls, Steps 14–20 and issues #25–#31. Before Step 21 starts, a pre-build delta check checks every assumption about plan 01 (rows A1–A54) against the shipped code with git grep. A failing row counts as a planning defect. Plans 02 and 03 are then parked, the failing rows go into the morning summary, and the steps that depend on them come back to redline. No developer patches one mid-run.

Evidence before content

Because the rule exists in three copies, a changed rule could leave the generator and the validator quietly disagreeing. Step 21 therefore moves the rule into one owner, adds one answer check and puts the independent checker in place, all without changing content. A committed script freezes today's content and generation requests at the step base. Code written later cannot adjust that record to fit a bug.

One saved-file bump, with version 2 frozen first

Plan 01's fingerprint treats any new key or value as a change to the saved shape. Step 22 is a pure refactor that freezes the version 2 reader. Step 23 then lands the final version 3 in one step, so an interim file is only ever unchanged v2 or final v3. Choices that haven't been built yet are refused where they apply. They are never quietly swapped for other content.

Look at these first

Three defaults with the biggest effect on a parent's page.

You can skim everything else. These three decide which problems a child sees by default. I chose all of them on your behalf, and each one is open to change.

D-bonds-tenDefault Number Bonds pages skip make-ten and crossing-ten

By default, no Number Bonds problem needs carrying or borrowing, so none makes or crosses ten. Within 10, no problem uses 10 as its whole. Within 20, the only whole of 20 is 10 and 10. "Include problems that carry or borrow" adds them back. The ruling, which settled the former open D22, is recorded. Three parts of how I applied it are my reading, though, not its words:

  1. Pictures are filtered as well as sentences.
  2. Make-ten problems count too, since 3 + 7 = 10 regroups in the ones column.
  3. It is a separate Number Bonds setting that mirrors Dry Math's choice. It is not one shared switch.
D-bonds-ten -> confirm a, b, c

D17Carrying means every problem carries or borrows

The regrouping choice has two options. "Without carrying or borrowing" gives today's page. "Every problem carries or borrows" makes each addition carry and each subtraction borrow at least once, never goes below zero and stays within the chosen range. There is no mixed page. Addition within 5 can't carry at all, so that choice is reported unavailable with a remedy that works.

D17 -> add a mixed mode

D21One blank, and every number at least 1

Wholes run from 2 to the chosen range (5, 10 or 20), every part is at least 1, and each problem has exactly one blank. A sentence can hide an addend, the starting number (? − 3 = 5) or the number taken away, but never the result. A picture hides a part, never the whole. The plan lists this as an open redline question.

D21 -> allow zero parts

D18What belongs to a fact family

What
A multiplication fact belongs to family f when either factor is f. A division fact belongs when the divisor or the answer is f. 0 appears as a factor inside every chosen family (5 × 0, 0 × 5). 0 ÷ 5 = 0 belongs to both the 5s and the 0s, so the 0s alone give 0 ÷ 1 … 0 ÷ 12. Nothing ever divides by 0.
Why
It is the natural inverse of your P3 families, with the zero rule stated once.
Tweak
Leave out ×0 facts unless the 0s are chosen.

Risks worth knowing

What could go wrong, and what already guards against it.

  • Plan 01 ships a different name, test or shape than this plan assumes.The pre-build delta check runs all 54 assumption rows before Step 21. A failing row parks plans 02 and 03 and returns to redline, and no developer ever patches it mid-run (D56).
  • Step 23 or Step 25 runs out of iterations, and the halt parks everything after it (P7).Step 22 takes the freeze, the aliases and the generic upgrade first. The answer check lands in Step 21, the upgrade prose moves to Step 24, and every step is sized for three rounds (D-split, D39, D63).
  • The checker shares a bug with production and passes it.It uses a different method by construction, an import scan enforces the separation, and anchors come from an independent script with known-garbage calibration (DD1, D9).
  • A refactor or a later slice quietly changes earlier content.Content and request hashes are captured by script at the base, known vectors are committed per new kind, and the version policy lives in CONTRIBUTING.md (D10, D58, D12).
  • A parent expects make-ten bonds on a default Number Bonds page.The help says so in plain words, one choice adds them back, and M2 records whether parents expected them. Widening the default is a one-line tweak (D-bonds-ten).
  • An interim build meets a saved choice it can't produce yet.The choice is refused only where it applies, shown without a reset and written back unchanged, and interim builds never touch real data (D26, D59, item 5).
  • Runtime reviewers see a fictional file that an earlier reviewer left behind, instead of the empty state.Each UI step's Problem confirms by screenshot only when the default selection is visible, and names the specs that prove everything else (D-flags).
  • The Dry Math answer line is too short for a three-digit handwritten answer.No automated metric can measure a text line, so M1 is the only check. Automated rows prove each row stays on one line and fits the page (D40, MU5).
  • Ubuntu CI prints a blank as a missing-glyph box.Blanks are drawn elements, key lines write ? in ASCII, and × and ÷ exist in every candidate font (D40, D52).
  • Plan 03 re-renders a standard Long sheet in large print and it overflows.This plan proves fit only at each sheet's own scale and states that as a fact for plan 03 to handle (D55).
