What a parent can do now

Choose a table, print it, check it

  • Pick Operation Grid as the sixth worksheet card, then the operation: Addition x + y, Multiplication x × y, or Powers x^y.
  • Set rows and columns from 2 to 12 each (10 × 10 to start) and choose how anchors are placed: Random, or Diagonal first on a square addition or multiplication grid.
  • Type a pre-filled count, or tap a preset: Nearly blank (10%), Half (50%), Mostly filled (75%), or Diagonal. Presets round down and always leave at least one box to fill.
  • Ask for something the page cannot hold and get a refusal that names the one limit in the way: powers stop at bases 1–10 and exponents 1–3 (no cell above 1,000), and large print stops at 10 × 10.
  • Print a 12 × 12 grid on one Letter or A4 page at standard scale, plus a key page with every cell filled in.
  • Save the choices as worksheet defaults; nothing else writes them, and older saved files load unchanged.
