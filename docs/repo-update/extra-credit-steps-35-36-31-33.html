What a parent can do now

Two new worksheets and one print for the whole set

  • Pick Clock Explorer for four different on-the-hour clocks, 1:00 to 12:00, every dial fully numbered with a long minute hand at 12 and a shorter, heavier hour hand. The child writes the hour; the key names the time and where each hand points.
  • Pick Tally Detective for four different totals from 1 to 20 drawn as tally marks, each full five crossed through. The child counts and writes the total; the key shows the grouping, such as 8 = 5 + 3.
  • Neither new worksheet has further choices: only the nickname, answer key, paper and print scale apply, and four cards always fill one page.
  • Press Add to packet under any preview to queue that exact sheet, for this child or another one, up to ten sheets. Reorder or remove them in the Printable packet panel; a sheet already in the packet cannot be added twice.
  • Choose one paper size for the whole packet and where the answer keys go, then Preview packet to see every page in print order and Print packet once. No sheet is regenerated, each keeps its own print scale, and every key heading names its sheet.
  • The packet lives only in the open page: nothing is saved, a reload empties it, and printing never clears it. Deleting a profile tells you how many of its sheets are queued, and they stay, labeled for a removed profile.
