What a parent can do now

Choose a tougher packet

Random packet now offers Challenge and Expert beside Easier, Medium, and Harder. Packet difficulty starts from safe built-in choices, not a child profile or the parent's saved family settings.

Use broader Dry Math

Create three-digit addition and subtraction, two-digit × one-digit multiplication, exact three-digit ÷ one-digit division, or division with a nonzero remainder. Answer spaces and keys follow each form.

Move beyond 1–10 grids

Operation Grid headers can stay in order or mix deterministically, and either axis can start above 1. Headers end at 99; sums and products remain bounded at 999.
